💡 Procurement Decision Insights

Use price, spending and risk information together to support evidence-based procurement decisions.

💰 Price Comparison

Compare prices offered by different suppliers for the same item or material before selecting a vendor.

📊 Average Price

The average unit price provides a reference point for identifying unusually high or low supplier prices.

📈 Spending Trends

Monthly spending patterns can help procurement teams identify increases, decreases and changing purchasing behaviour.

⚠️ Risk-Aware Selection

Procurement decisions should consider both supplier price and predicted vendor risk rather than price alone.

🎯 Procurement Recommendation

Use this analysis together with the AI vendor risk prediction and vendor performance information. A supplier offering the lowest price may not always be the most suitable option if the supplier has poor performance or high procurement risk.
