System Capabilities

Key capabilities of the AI Procurement Decision Support System.

🤖 AI-Based Risk Prediction

Evaluate procurement vendors and identify suppliers that may represent low, medium or high procurement risk.

📊 Vendor Analytics

Analyse vendor performance using price, quality, delivery, reliability and other procurement indicators.

💰 Price Analysis

Compare supplier prices and identify price differences to support evidence-based procurement decisions.

📈 Procurement Trends

Analyse procurement spending patterns and transaction activity over time.

🛒 Transaction Management

Record procurement transactions and use transaction information for procurement analytics.

📑 Decision Support Reports

Provide procurement teams with consolidated analytics to support informed supplier and purchasing decisions.

💡 Procurement Decision Support

Use the system's AI predictions and analytics to support data-driven procurement decisions.

Important: Vendor price should not be considered independently when selecting a supplier. The procurement team can combine vendor price, vendor performance, procurement risk, and historical transaction information to make more informed procurement decisions.
